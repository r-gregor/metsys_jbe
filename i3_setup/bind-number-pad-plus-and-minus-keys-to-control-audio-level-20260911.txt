To bind the plus (+) and minus (-) keys on your number pad to control volume in the i3 window manager, you
need to use their X11 keysym names: KP_Add and KP_Subtract.  Open your i3 configuration file (usually located

at ~/.config/i3/config) and add the following lines depending on your audio system:
For PulseAudio / PipeWire (Recommended)
Most modern Linux distributions use pactl for audio control.

[i3config]
# Raise volume (Numpad +)
bindcode KP_Add exec --no-startup-id pactl set-sink-volume @DEFAULT_SINK@ +5%

# Lower volume (Numpad -)
bindcode KP_Subtract exec --no-startup-id pactl set-sink-volume @DEFAULT_SINK@ -5%

For ALSA
If your system relies strictly on ALSA, use amixer instead:

[i3config]
# Raise volume (Numpad +)
bindcode KP_Add exec --no-startup-id amixer -q sset Master 5%+

# Lower volume (Numpad -)
bindcode KP_Subtract exec --no-startup-id amixer -q sset Master 5%-

Key Steps to Apply Changes
  * Save the configuration file.
  * Restart i3 in place by pressing Mod4 + Shift + r (or Mod1 + Shift + r, depending on your modifier key).
