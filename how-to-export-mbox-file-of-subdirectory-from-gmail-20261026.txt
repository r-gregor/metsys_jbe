Q: how to export mbox file of a subfolder from google mail?

AI Overview
To export an MBOX file for a specific Gmail subfolder or label, use Google Takeout with label selection or
connect your account via an email client like Mozilla Thunderbird.

Method 1: Using Google Takeout[**1] (Selective Labels/Folders)
Google Takeout exports Gmail folders as individual .mbox files corresponding to your Gmail labels.
01. Go to the Google Takeout page and sign in with your Google account.
02. Click "Deselect all" near the top of the list.
03. Scroll down to Mail and check the box next to it.
04. Click the button that says "All mail data included" (or the gray option below Mail).
05. In the pop-up window, uncheck "All Mail".
06. Scroll down and check only the specific label or subfolder you want to export. Click OK.
07. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click Next step.
08. Choose your delivery method (e.g., Send download link via email), set the file type to .zip, and choose
    your archive size.
09. Click Create export.
10. Wait for Google to process the archive (this can take from a few minutes to several hours), open the
    download link sent to your email, and unzip the downloaded file. Inside the Takeout/Mail/ folder, you will
    find the .mbox file named after your label.

Method 2: Using Mozilla Thunderbird (IMAP + ImportExportTools NG)
If you want instant selective control without waiting for Google Takeout:
01. Install Mozilla Thunderbird and configure your Gmail account via IMAP so your folders and subfolders sync
    locally.
02. Install the free ImportExportTools NG add-on inside Thunderbird.
03. Right-click the specific Gmail subfolder in the left-hand panel of Thunderbird.
04. Select ImportExportTools NG -> Export folder as mbox file, then choose your computer destination.

[**1]
Google Takeout is a free service created by the Google Data Liberation Front that lets you export and download
a copy of your data from Google products.

How to Use Google Takeout
01. Go to the Page: Log into your Google Account and visit the Google Takeout page.
02. Select Your Data: By default, all Google services holding your data are checked. Click Deselect all if you
    only want specific items (such as Gmail, Google Photos, or Drive), then check the boxes next to the
    services you want.  03. Choose Delivery Settings: Click Next step and select how you want to receive your
    archive:
    - Delivery method: Receive a download link via email, or add the archive directly to Google Drive,
      Dropbox, OneDrive, or Box.
    - Frequency: Choose a one-time export or recurring exports (every 2 months for 1 year).
    - File type and size: Pick .zip or .tgz and set a maximum file size (from 1 GB up to 50 GB).
04. Create Export: Click Create export. Depending on how much data you have, the process can take anywhere
    from a few minutes to several days.
05. Download: Once you receive the notification email, click the link to download your archive files to your
    device.

Are you trying to backup a specific service (like Google Photos or Gmail) or transfer your data to a different
account?

To back up a specific service, you can isolate it during the selection process to save time and storage space.

Step-by-Step Selection
01. Go to Takeout: Open the Google Takeout page.
02. Clear the Default List: Click the Deselect all button near the top of the list. This unchecks all 50+
    Google services.
03. Select Your Service: Scroll down the alphabetical list and check the box next to the exact service you
    need.

Customizing Your Export
Many popular services allow you to filter exactly what gets backed up before you create the archive:
* Google Photos: Click All photo albums included to uncheck specific years or folders you do not need.
* Google Drive: Click All Drive data included to deselect specific folders, or click Multiple formats to
  choose how your docs convert (e.g., converting Google Docs to Microsoft Word format).
* Gmail: Click All Mail data included to back up specific labels or folders instead of your entire inbox.

Once you have customized your service, scroll to the bottom, click Next step, and choose your download method.
